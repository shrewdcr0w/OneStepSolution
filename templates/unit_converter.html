IEC Standard Definitions

Binary Contractions:

  • Mebi = Mega + Binary
  • Gibi = Giga + Binary
  • Tebi = Tera + Binary

The Math:

  • MB = 1,0002 (Metric)
  • MiB = 1,0242 (Binary)
  • Ratio = ~1.048x Difference

*Standardized by the IEC to resolve the 1000 vs 1024 byte conflict.
